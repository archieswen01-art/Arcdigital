About

One developer, a small team, a lot of shipped software.

We're a small studio based in Liberia building the software that schools, clinics, shops and community groups actually need — no bloat, no seat licenses, no vendor lock-in.

ArcDigital started with a simple frustration. Schools were running on spreadsheets, paper registers and WhatsApp groups — losing track of fees, grades, and students who needed help. The software available was either too expensive, too generic, or built for a country that wasn't ours.

So we built our own. ArcDigital now powers 32 modules across schools in Liberia — enrolment, fees, results, attendance, ID cards, timetables, AI-graded quizzes, transcripts, elections, chat. Real working software, used daily.

From there we applied the same principles to other industries: a hospital system for a clinic that had no way to track patients. An HR tool for a business drowning in paperwork. A POS for a restaurant that lost money every time the till ran out of receipt paper. Every product line in this portfolio exists because someone needed it.

We ship the full source code. We deploy on infrastructure you can afford. We stay available after launch. That's the whole promise.
